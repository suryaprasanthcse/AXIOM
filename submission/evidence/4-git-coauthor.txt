Initial import of the Zero to Shipped build, Phase 1 plus its pivots.

  (12/12 live) and 20 unit tests
- Pivots: CloudFront dropped (new-account restriction), Bedrock dropped
  (Free plan) in favour of a deterministic plain-text proof report;
  arena cache and daily cap on paid Access Analyzer checks
